What Is a Chatbot and What Does It Bring to Your Business?

A chatbot is a software system that interacts with users through text or voice conversation interfaces. As of 2026, the old "if-else" rule-based bots have been largely replaced by intelligent assistants built on GPT-4o, Claude and similar large language models (LLMs).

A well-designed chatbot provides your business with:

  • Automatic responses to 65–80% of customer questions
  • 24/7 uninterrupted customer service capacity
  • Reducing average response time from minutes to seconds
  • Increasing employee productivity (time savings for repetitive questions)
  • More customer touchpoints (WhatsApp, web, mobile, phone)

Chatbot Types: Which One Is Right for You?

📋

Rule-Based Chatbot

Operates according to a pre-written scenario tree. Suitable for simple and predictable questions. Low cost, limited flexibility.

🧠

GPT / LLM-Based Chatbot

Works with a large language model, understands natural language, tracks context. Answers complex questions. High performance, slightly higher cost.

🗂️

RAG-Based Chatbot

Generates answers based on your company documents, catalog and FAQ data. Ideal for accurate answers based on your own data.

🔗

CRM/ERP-Integrated Chatbot

An advanced assistant that talks to your existing business systems (order status, customer records, inventory) and converts to action. The highest ROI category.

5 Stages of Chatbot Development

1. Scope and Scenario Design

This is the most critical stage. It documents in detail which questions the chatbot will answer, in which situations it will route to human support, and which actions it will perform (creating orders, filling forms, etc.). A poorly designed scenario makes even the most powerful model useless.

2. Data Preparation and Knowledge Base Creation

For a GPT or RAG-based chatbot, company FAQ documents, product catalog, service descriptions and frequently asked question-answer pairs are prepared. This data quality directly affects the chatbot's accuracy rate.

3. Model Selection and Fine-Tuning

Depending on the use case, a choice is made between OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude or open-source (Llama, Mistral) models. If sensitive or sector-specific data is involved, the model is customized with fine-tuning or RAG architecture.

4. Integration and Testing

Website widget, WhatsApp API, CRM or ERP integrations are set up. Comprehensive testing is done with real user scenarios. The incorrect response rate is reduced to an acceptable level.

5. Go-Live and Optimization

After the chatbot goes live, conversation logs are reviewed regularly in the first 30 days. The knowledge base is updated for frequently incorrect questions, and response quality is continuously improved.

How to Design a KVKK-Compliant Chatbot?

When chatbots collect personal data from users, obligations arise under KVKK. These elements are mandatory for a KVKK-compliant chatbot:

  • Disclosure text: The user must be informed of what data is being collected when the chatbot starts
  • Explicit consent: User approval must be obtained for personal data processing
  • Data minimization: Only necessary data should be collected
  • Deletion request flow: Users must be able to request deletion of their data
  • Data location: If possible, data should be stored on servers in Turkey

With on-premise deployment, a chatbot can be created that runs on your own infrastructure without data leaving the premises. This is especially a critical requirement for finance, health and public sector.
