SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of improving your website's visibility in organic (unpaid) search results on Google, Bing and other search engines. In 2026, SEO remains the most sustainable traffic channel for businesses.

⚡ Summary: SEO = solid technical foundation + valuable content + trust signals.

What Is SEO and How Does It Work?

Search engines crawl, index and rank billions of pages. SEO optimizes your site to rank higher. Three main layers:

  • Technical SEO: Speed, mobile-friendliness, HTTPS, sitemap, Core Web Vitals
  • On-page SEO: Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal links
  • Off-page SEO: Backlinks, brand mentions, local SEO

Why SEO Matters for Businesses

  • Organic traffic: Continuous visitors without ad spend
  • Trust and authority: Top rankings strengthen brand perception
  • High conversion: Searchers already have intent
  • Long-term ROI: Well-ranked content drives traffic for months
