Native or Cross-Platform?

This question sits at the center of every mobile project discussion. As of 2026, three main approaches stand out:

Native

Swift / Kotlin

Swift for iOS, Kotlin for Android. Highest performance and deepest platform integration. Requires a separate team for each platform.

Cross-Platform

Flutter

Google's Dart-based framework. One codebase for iOS + Android + Web. Performance close to native; excellent animations.

Cross-Platform

React Native

JavaScript/TypeScript-based and familiar to web developers. Backed by Meta with a large ecosystem. Code sharing with web is a major advantage.

Which Technology Should You Choose?

  • Choose Flutter: When launching a new app, working with a limited budget, and wanting consistent UI on iOS and Android. In 2026, it is the most balanced option for speed and cost.
  • Choose React Native: When your team knows JavaScript and you want to share code with an existing web project.
  • Choose native: For high-budget apps that need deep access to platform features such as camera, ARKit/ARCore, or highly complex animations.

Mobile App Development Process: 6 Steps

  1. Discovery and scoping (1–2 weeks): Target audience, competitor analysis, core features (MVP), and technical requirements are defined. Skipping this stage leads to costly revisions later.
  2. UI/UX design (2–4 weeks): Wireframes, prototypes, and final Figma designs are produced. User testing should happen at this stage.
  3. Backend development (parallel, 4–12 weeks): API services, database structure, and third-party integrations (payments, notifications, location, etc.) are built.
  4. Mobile app development (4–16 weeks): The app is built with Flutter or React Native and integrated with the backend.
  5. QA and testing (2–4 weeks): Comprehensive testing across devices and OS versions. Beta testing with real users.
  6. Launch (1 week): Submissions to the App Store and Google Play. Apple review takes 1–3 days; Google Play is usually approved within hours.

5 Critical Points to Keep in Mind

  • Adopt an MVP approach: You do not need every feature in the first release. Validate your core value proposition, then scale.
  • Offline support: Internet connectivity is not stable everywhere in Turkey. Offline capability for core functions significantly improves user experience.
  • Push notification strategy: Excessive notifications are the #1 reason apps get uninstalled. Build a smart, personalized notification strategy.
  • App Store Optimization (ASO): Title, description, keywords, and screenshots must be optimized so users can discover your app in the store.
  • Analytics integration: You cannot improve what you do not measure—track user behavior with Firebase Analytics or Amplitude.

Flutter vs React Native: What Ankara Developers Prefer

As of 2026, Flutter is chosen in 58% of mobile development projects in Ankara. Main reasons include:

  • Single codebase: One codebase for iOS, Android, and web.
  • Easy to learn Dart: You can get started without Java/Kotlin or Swift experience.
  • Performance: Near-native performance; especially strong for animation-heavy apps.
  • Google backing: Active development by Google provides long-term reliability.

React Native remains ideal for teams that know JavaScript/TypeScript and want to bring existing web skills to mobile. Meta's New Architecture update has largely resolved earlier performance concerns.

Standing Out on the App Store and Google Play: ASO Guide

Building a great app alone is not enough to drive downloads. App Store Optimization (ASO) improves organic discoverability:

  • Title: Include your primary target keyword (max. 30 characters).
  • Subtitle / short description: Place secondary keywords here.
  • Screenshots: The first three screenshots should convey your most critical value propositions.
  • Ratings and reviews: Regular updates and fast review responses positively affect store algorithms.
  • Localization: Turkish ASO directly affects organic ranking in the Turkey App Store.

Will a Flutter app be accepted on the App Store?

Yes. Flutter apps deliver fully native look and performance; Apple and Google accept them without issue. Thousands of major brands use Flutter.

How much does mobile app maintenance cost?

Budget 15–20% of annual development cost for iOS and Android OS updates, new device compatibility, and security patches. Plan for an average of 3,000–10,000 TL/month.

Is KVKK compliance mandatory for mobile apps?

Yes. All mobile apps that process personal data of Turkish users are subject to KVKK (Turkey's data protection law). Explicit consent, data retention policy, and secure data transfer are required.

Is starting with an MVP the right strategy?

In most cases, yes. An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) approach protects you from large investments without market validation. Launching with core features and shaping the product from user feedback has become the standard approach for startups.
