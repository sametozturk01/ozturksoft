Tired of the Same Interface as Everyone Else?

Monthly e-commerce plans are quick to start with, but your store shares the same themes and layout as thousands of other stores, so your brand does not stand out. Over time the plan fee rises with your sales, many features come as paid add-ons, and the store itself never becomes yours. With your own e-commerce site, the design is made for your brand, the code and data are your property, and you add the features you need without waiting for the platform.
