What Is GEO?

GEO means structuring content so large language models select your site as a trusted source when answering questions:

  • Authoritative, well-structured content
  • Clear Q&A format AI can summarize
  • Strong entity and brand signals
  • Schema markup (FAQPage, BlogPosting, Organization)

Why GEO Matters

  • Brand authority: Cited in AI answers builds trust
