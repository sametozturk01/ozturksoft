7 Key Factors That Determine Price

1. Design Originality

There is a significant difference between working on a ready-made WordPress theme and building UI/UX design from scratch. A custom Figma design means at least 1–2 extra weeks of work. If your budget is tight, high-quality themes are a sensible starting point. But if you want to stand out from competitors, custom design is essential.

2. Technology Stack

Ready-made systems like WordPress and Webflow offer speed and cost advantages, but they are limited when it comes to scalability. Modern technologies such as React + Node.js or Next.js provide a more flexible structure in the long run, though development time and cost increase.

3. Number of Pages and Content

Each page means separate SEO optimization, content placement, and quality testing. The price difference between a 5-page site and a 50-page site is roughly linear.

4. E-Commerce Integrations

Virtual POS integration (iyzico, PayTR, Garanti, etc.), shipping integration, and connections to ERP and accounting software require serious technical work. Budget an average of 3.000–8.000 TL extra per integration.

5. SEO and Performance Optimization

For your site to rank well on Google, you need technical SEO, page speed optimization (Core Web Vitals), schema markup, and a content strategy. Choosing an agency that includes these services is far more cost-effective than paying for them separately later.

6. Multilingual Support

A bilingual Turkish and English site means roughly %40–60 more work in content, SEO, and development compared to a single-language site.

7. Maintenance and Update Package

After handover, we recommend adding a monthly maintenance package of 1.500–5.000 TL for security updates, content changes, and technical support.

Agency or Freelancer?

There is no single right answer; each option has advantages and drawbacks:

  • Freelancer: Usually lower cost and faster communication. On large-scale projects, however, one person's capacity may fall short.
  • Small/Mid-Size Agency: Brings design, development, SEO, and content expertise under one roof. Prices are typically %30–50 higher, but coordination burden is lower.
  • Large Corporate Agency: Brand power and large teams are available, but costs and processes can be heavy. Often disproportionately expensive for small and mid-size businesses.

What to Look for When Having a Website Built in Ankara

  1. Review the portfolio: Always test sites the agency or freelancer has built before. Check mobile compatibility, speed, and design quality.
  2. Is SEO included? Most firms that only say "we build websites" do not include technical SEO in the proposal scope.
  3. Do you own the source code? At delivery, make sure all source code and domain/hosting access are transferred to you.
  4. Sign a contract: Delivery date, number of revisions, and price must be fixed in a written contract.
  5. Ask for references: Firms that let you speak directly with past clients stand out for reliability.

Factors Raising Ankara Website Costs in 2026

Exchange Rate Fluctuations

Most software development tools, hosting services, and licensed software are priced in USD or EUR. In 2026, the dollar exchange rate has increased website costs by %60–80 compared to 2023. For this reason, some agencies now quote prices indexed to foreign currency.

The Changing Role of AI Tools

AI tools such as ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, and Midjourney have sped up development processes but also shifted the cost of quality consulting. Templates produced with AI may look cheap, but custom business logic, SEO optimization, and brand identity still require expert hands.

Core Web Vitals Standards

As Google has applied Core Web Vitals more strongly as a ranking factor in 2024–2026, performance optimization is no longer optional—it is mandatory. Improving LCP, FID, and CLS scores requires additional technical work.

10 Questions to Ask When Choosing an Agency

  1. Will the website's source code and all files be delivered to me?
  2. Will domain and hosting accounts be opened in my name?
  3. Is technical SEO (meta tags, schema markup, sitemap) included?
  4. On which devices will mobile compatibility tests be run?
  5. How many revisions do I have after delivery?
  6. Are Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS) values guaranteed?
  7. Is Google Analytics and Search Console setup included?
  8. Will the site comply with KVKK (Turkish data protection law)?
  9. For how long will security updates be provided?
  10. Can you provide customer references from your previous projects?

Summary: How Much Budget Should You Set?

If you are a startup or small business, you can get a solid corporate brochure site for 15.000–35.000 TL. If you are targeting e-commerce, 50.000 TL and above is a realistic starting point for infrastructure that will affect sales especially in the first year.

Do not limit your budget to development cost alone. Factor in domain, hosting, SSL, SEO, and maintenance. Evaluating annual total cost of ownership (TCO) helps you avoid surprise bills later.
